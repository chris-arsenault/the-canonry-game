# Relationship Catalog Design

Working document from the design session on relationship catalog for Ice Remembers MVP. Category-pair granularity (engine relationships are defined at EntityCategory, not kind — source file: `src/Engine/Kinds/RelationshipKindDefinition.cs`).

## Framing — relationships vs typed effects (read this first)

**Relationships = graph topology.** They describe what is connected to what. Actions and generators use them for: selection filters (`has_relationship`, `lacks_relationship`, `shares_related`, `relationship_count`, `graph_path`), variable resolution (walk edges to find related entities), wiring (produce edges as part of bundles), and mutation (`create_relationship`, `archive_relationship`). Pattern confirmed from the-canonry's `apps/canonry/webui/public/default-project/actions.json` and `generators.json` — same design family.

**Typed effects = attribute modifiers defined on entities.** An artifact may grant a stacking buff to its wielder. A region may grant effects to anyone at its hexes. These effects live on the **source entity** (the artifact, the region) and flow to affected entities along topological connections (a `Holds` edge, hex containment). Effect stacking follows the Pathfinder 2e rules in CLAUDE.md Principle #10 — same-type take best, cross-type stack.

**The relationship edge is the path, not the payload.** The `Holds` edge does not "carry" a typed effect as a property of the edge kind; the artifact carries its effects, and `Holds` is the edge the effect flows through. When this document describes how a relationship is used by the simulation, the concrete uses are filtering/targeting/mutation — not "carries a typed effect."

## Processes: ~30 domain-authored verbs with explicit narrative intent

The process catalog is authored in `src/Engine/Domain/IceRemembers/IceRemembersSemanticDefinition.cs`. Each verb is a specific, chosen thing with a named narrative meaning: `SettlementClaimsEcho` is "the settlement absorbs a remembered name into civic legitimacy," `ArtifactCorrupt` is "the artifact drinks the region's pristiness into its own raw power," `PenguinWield` is "the penguin pulls the artifact's working power into their own echo." These narrative intents are the first-class design objects; everything below is scaffolding that helped verify completeness.

**The 10 cross-entity conversion verbs** (the only verbs that target another entity — the RPS ring):

*+1 ring (adjacent predator):*
- `SettlementClaimsEcho` — Settlement drains NPC Echo → Settlement Dominion
- `FactionExtort` — Faction drains Settlement Shelter → Faction Reach
- `RegionFortify` — Region drains Faction Provisions → Region Holding
- `ArtifactCorrupt` — Artifact drains Region Pristiness → Artifact Power
- `PenguinWield` — Penguin drains Artifact Power → Penguin Echo

*+2 ring (skip predator):*
- `FactionStarve` — Faction drains NPC Warmth → Faction Provisions
- `RegionReclaim` — Region drains Settlement Dominion → Region Pristiness
- `ArtifactBind` — Artifact drains Faction Reach → Artifact Resonance
- `PenguinForage` — Penguin drains Region Holding → Penguin Warmth
- `SettlementSanctify` — Settlement drains Artifact Resonance → Settlement Shelter

**Other verb categories** (operate on self/hex, not another entity):
- Self-sacrifice: `ArtifactChannel`, `FactionAgitate`, `PenguinEndure`, `RegionCleanse`, `SettlementOverreach`
- Hex→Health inflow: `PenguinWarm`, `ArtifactRepair`, `FactionOrganize`, `RegionRoot`, `SettlementGrow`
- Untouched→Hex outflow: `PenguinWitness`, `ArtifactVivify`, `FactionDistribute`, `RegionBreach`, `SettlementStripRookeries`
- Mobility: `PenguinTravel`, `ArtifactCompel`, `SettlementDeliberate`, `FactionPreach`, `RegionCommune`
- Drift: `PenguinWander`, `ArtifactCirculate`, `SettlementGossip`, `FactionQuarrel`, `RegionDream`

Provider selection currently uses: primary-plane locality (within 1 hex on physical for NPC/Artifact, or semantic for Settlement/Faction/Region — the semantic plane is a separate hex grid with Autonomy/Resonance/Tradition axes, not attribute-space); attribute thresholds (source ≥ floor, destination ≤ ceiling); and `AttributeRef.Target(attr, kind)` to enumerate candidates.

**Relationship preconditions exist in code but are not yet used by any verb**: `TargetHasRelationshipToSelf(kind, minStrength)`, `SelfHasRelationshipToTarget(kind, minStrength)`, `RelatedEntityMatchesTarget(kind)` (`src/Engine/Processes/Preconditions.cs`). **Relationship-creating mutations exist but are not emitted**: `ModifyOrCreateRelationshipMutation`. Adding relationship awareness means extending the semantic-verb schema so specific verbs gain specific preconditions (e.g., `PenguinWield` requires `SelfHasRelationshipToTarget: holds`) and/or specific mutations (e.g., `ArtifactCorrupt` creates a `CorruptedBy` edge from region→artifact as part of its flow).

The **Process integration** column in the catalog below references specific verbs by name — which verbs would gate, score, or mutate on each relationship. Where the current verb set doesn't naturally use a relationship, I note that rather than inventing a fit.

## Stripped as location/semantic-derivable (do NOT count in the catalog)

- `LocatedAt` — agent's current hex; derivable from position.
- `StoredAt` — artifact's current hex; derivable from position.
- `Borders` — region adjacency; derivable from hex grid.
- `Contains` — region→settlement geometric containment; derivable from hex layout.
- `LoyalTo` — overlaps `MemberOf` + relationship strength (Principle #8).
- `InvolvedIn` — semantically null catch-all; delete rather than keep.

## Relationship catalog — narrative + concrete simulation use

Engine primitives the catalog entries reference:

- **Generator selection filters** — pick target entities by their graph state: `graph_path` (walk N hops matching kind/subtype/status), `has_relationship`, `lacks_relationship`, `relationship_count`.
- **Generator variable resolution** — look up existing entities by traversing edges from the target: `from.relatedTo: $target, relationshipKind: X, direction: in|out|both`.
- **Generator wiring** — create new edges as part of the produced bundle.
- **Generator saturation limits** — cap how many edges of a kind an entity has before the generator stops applying.
- **Action actor selection** — same filters/traversals as generators, applied to find who can act.
- **Action targeting filters** — `has_relationship`, `lacks_relationship`, `shares_related` (both endpoints share an edge to a third entity), `graph_path` asserts.
- **Action variable resolution** — same as generator variables; e.g., "find the previous owner" by traversing `controls` incoming.
- **Action outcome mutations** — `create_relationship`, `archive_relationship`, `archive_all_relationships`, `change_status` (which cascades edge lifecycle).
- **Process preconditions** — `TargetHasRelationshipToSelf`, `SelfHasRelationshipToTarget`, `RelatedEntityMatchesTarget` (available in code; currently not emitted by any verb).
- **Process mutations** — `ModifyOrCreateRelationshipMutation` (available in code; currently not emitted by any verb).
- **Narration verbs** — edge-formation/ending produces readable text via `verbs.formed/ended/inverseFormed/inverseEnded`.
- **Historical persistence** — non-cullable edges survive target ending.
- **Exclusivity / symmetry / polarity / cullable / decayRate** — edge kind metadata read by culling, narration, and filter logic.

| 20 | **AtWarWith** | A×A (faction↔faction in practice; engine enforces at category level) | Formal active war between factions — distinct from `RivalOf`'s background peer-hostility. | **Symmetric**, **negative**, cullable (slow decay — wars don't end on their own quickly but aren't immortal). **Action targeting**: `sue_for_peace` and `claim_victory` actions exclusively target factions the actor is at-war-with (pattern directly from canonry's `actions.json`). `lacks_relationship: at_war_with` gates trade / diplomacy / alliance actions. **Action outcomes**: declare-war creates AtWarWith with `bidirectional: true`; peace / victory / conquest archive it. **Generator selection filters**: "factions AtWarWith for N ticks" triggers war-weary bundles (refugee flows, war-hero emergence, peace-broker arrivals — matches canonry's `war_weary` tag pattern). | 21 | **BetrayedBy** | A×A (faction→faction in practice) | A named, permanent record of betrayal — never forgotten, survives leadership change. | **Asymmetric negative**, **non-cullable** (`decayRate: "none"` in canonry). **Action targeting**: `lacks_relationship: betrayed_by` gates alliance / trust-requiring actions — factions can't ally with their known betrayers without ceremonial absolution. **Action outcomes**: betrayal-class actions create BetrayedBy as an additional persistent outcome alongside whatever other state change (alliance breakage, war declaration) — the betrayal is *separate* state from the triggering event. Narrative-weight gate rather than a hard block; could be scoring penalty instead of precondition. No role in the hostile verbs (betrayal doesn't affect whether you corrupt or starve — that's `AtWarWith` / `RivalOf` territory). |

## Open design work

- **Decay rate policy per edge** — pick between canonry's categorical `slow / medium / none` and the-canonry-game's numeric cull threshold, then assign per edge.
- **Narration verbs per edge** — every edge needs formed / ended / inverseFormed / inverseEnded strings.
- **Cullable / non-cullable classification per edge** — lineage and memorial edges non-cullable; alliance / rivalry / trade cullable.
- **Process-schema extension** — semantic-verb schema needs fields for relationship preconditions and relationship-creating mutations so the cross-entity verbs in the catalog above can actually emit what their Process integration entries describe.
