# Deep Ice Corruption

*Archivist's register: pressure entry.*

Deep ice corruption is the name given to phenomena that spread from old strata
through opened shafts, fissures, thermal veins, sealed corridors, artifacts,
ritual sites, and the administrative habit of not asking what a profitable vein
is connected to. The name is convenient. It may also be too moral.

The corruption does not behave like an enemy. It behaves like an inheritance
finding available space.

## Signs

Records name many symptoms: freeze-enthrall, crystal under nail-beds, fungi-frost
in wounds, light consumed by repaired vault sections, survey lines changing
between observations, pressure-depth anomalies eating fire-core output, and
places where a sealed shaft ends observation rather than activity.

The Glow-Fissure's pulse, shaft eight's heat hunger, the Obelisk's rising, the
Medallion's three-two rhythm, and the deep-fissure events near combined
operations corridors may not be one phenomenon. They are similar enough that the
distinction has not comforted anyone who noticed it.

## The Pattern

Containment gaps matter. Attention matters. Secrecy matters. Extraction matters.
The corruption accelerates where structures are opened, abandoned, renamed, or
declared solved. It is patient because geological things are patient and because
whatever attends through the deep may not experience time as a sequence of
emergencies.

## Chronicle Use

Use deep ice corruption as consequence without simple guilt. Let reforms be
locally correct and cumulatively dangerous. Let sealed doors hold for decades and
still fail. Let characters discover that not watching a thing is not the same as
stopping it.
