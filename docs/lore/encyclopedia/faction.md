# Faction

A named organization, order, compact, cell, or choir. Factions are semantic
and territorial actors: they project doctrine, loyalty, control, and conflict
without needing to physically walk the map like penguins.

## Subtypes

- `order`
- `compact`
- `cell`
- `choir`

## Attributes

- `cohesion`: health; reaches zero and the faction becomes historical.
- `reach`: outward projection and operational span.
- `provisions`: internal material reserve. Cultural display names may differ:
  aurora light, fire-cores, offerings.

## Low-Altitude Behavior

Factions move on the semantic plane through preaching, quarrel, and other
doctrinal shifts. On the physical plane they exert influence, consolidate
influence, reduce hostile influence, expand controlled coverage rarely, and
may move their seat.

## High-Altitude Role

Factions are common actors for gambits such as declaring war, betrayal, warding,
or control changes. Generators may create splinter factions, orders, compact
fragments, or factions tied to new settlements and regions.
