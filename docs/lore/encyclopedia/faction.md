# Faction

*Archivist's register: civic entry, general.*

A faction is any named body that can make a penguin or pod act differently than
they would alone. Some factions hold halls and seals. Some occupy one tunnel and
three blackmail ledgers. Some are choirs, compacts, orders, cells, guilds,
parliaments, courier chains, or hunting circles. A faction is not important
because it is formal. It is important because others must account for it.

The Berg's factions rarely begin as villains. They begin as answers: who keeps
the fire moving, who remembers the names, who guards the route, who speaks when
the council cannot, who knows which tunnel is still safe. Then the answer
hardens, and penguins begin living inside it.

## Common Sorts

- **Orders** claim continuity, doctrine, and trained practice.
- **Compacts** are agreements given a body: signatures, hostages, couriers,
  ration lines, and consequences.
- **Cells** are small enough to deny and large enough to change events.
- **Choirs** act through shared song, ritual, cadence, and obedience.

The Icebound Exchange is a faction because ledgers can starve a colony. The
Midnight Claws are a faction because routes answer to them before they answer to
law. The Wake-Singers are a faction because belief can coordinate a war-pod more
tightly than any signal flag.

## How Factions Fail

Factions become dangerous when their method survives their reason. Aurora
procedure can distribute suffering with the same grace it once used to
distribute plenty. Nightshelf secrecy can preserve a people or consume them from
inside. Orca liturgy can hold a pod together while spending shamans faster than
the deep can return them.

A faction may collapse by schism, confession, silence, exposure, starvation,
audit, betrayal, or simple exhaustion. It may also persist as a habit long after
its officers are dead. The ice has many records of extinct institutions whose
forms still move living flippers.

## Chronicle Use

Use factions as pressures with memory. Give them a doctrine, a practical service,
a private cost, and a thing they cannot admit without ceasing to be themselves.
When a faction acts, show both the penguins who benefit and the penguins ground
down by the benefit's machinery.
