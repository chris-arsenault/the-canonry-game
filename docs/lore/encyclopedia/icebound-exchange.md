# Icebound Exchange

*Archivist's register: faction entry.*

The Icebound Exchange is the Aurora Stack's great merchant institution: ledgers,
warehouses, lenses, auditors, contracts, debt marks, trade families, and the
polite violence of arithmetic. It tracks aurora-crystals, fire-cores, krill,
pressed-ice records, route fees, salvage claims, and obligations that have
outlived everyone who first incurred them.

The Exchange believes anything important can be entered into a column. History
has not disproved this. It has only made the columns uglier.

## Method

Deals are sealed under aurora-light and broken contracts are recorded where
melting cannot rescue the debtor. The Exchange can make trade possible between
parties that despise each other because it offers a third loyalty: the balance.

Its genius is administrative endurance. It can process shortfall, scandal,
contraband, death-shards, and wartime rationing without changing handwriting.
This is useful. It is also how the unbearable becomes routine before anyone has
decided whether routine is permitted.

## Influence

The Exchange sits in the Trade Terraces but reaches through the Accord, the
courier routes, the warming-chambers, and the council's appetite for clean
figures. Some leaders rise from its halls. Some never leave them in spirit.

## Chronicle Use

Use the Icebound Exchange when money, memory, and survival need the same ledger.
It is not merely greed. It is the civic belief that a counted obligation is safer
than an uncounted one, even when counting becomes its own form of harm.
