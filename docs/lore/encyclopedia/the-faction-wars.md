# The Faction Wars

*Archivist's register: era lens.*

The Faction Wars are remembered as conflict between penguins. The ice remembers
something colder: scarcity activating instruments that had already been built.
The systems did not fail. They functioned under conditions their makers had
called impossible and produced exactly what their shapes allowed.

The krill-blooms thinned. Fire-core access became leverage. The Accord allocated
deprivation with the same formality it had once used for surplus. Geography
stopped being the main boundary; doctrine, route access, silence, and ration
math cut through colonies and families instead.

## The War Of Working Systems

The Aurora Stack convened councils, held votes, filed audits, and watched crisis
deepen inside complete procedure. The Nightshelf sealed routes, enforced silence
laws, amended doctrine, and consumed reformers with the same discipline that had
once preserved survival.

The Smelt's burning turned raw fire-core control into dependency on processing.
Secret Accord drafts show renegotiation before public rupture. Shipment delays,
short-weighting, ration formulas, and armed escort became weapons with better
stationery.

## Sorting And Shadows

Ideology replaced geography. Some Aurora learned tunnel implication better than
public consensus. Some Nightshelf found open councils intoxicating and then
discovered that openness can also be performance. The Midnight Claws rose where
formal governance could not protect routes it still needed.

The Fire Keeper teaching remembered in early ice - what is shared is power
multiplied - was rewritten into its harsher descendant: what is hoarded is power
preserved. The old rhythm carried the new wound forward.

## The Third Pressure

Orca raids in this stratum are often recorded as savagery. Several were
responses to penguin expansion onto hunting grounds. The Wake-Singers pursued a
deeper objective while penguin factions fought over corridors: breach, descent,
and the long project of feeding what waited below.

The deep itself began answering pressure. Obelisks rose, survey lines shifted,
sealed strata released impressions, and maintenance requests outlived the
offices meant to process them.

## Useful Lore To Extract

- Scarcity should reveal institutions rather than merely break them.
- The Accord can be a weapon even while preventing total collapse.
- The Claws are born from abandoned governance, not from simple appetite.
- Silence laws and public procedure should each have their own cruelty.
- Orca claims may be retaliatory, territorial, theological, or all three.

## Chronicle Use

Use this era lens when a record needs competence becoming horror. Do not require
all later factional conflict to descend from this era by name. The portable
truth is that working machinery can become dangerous when no one can make it
stop.
