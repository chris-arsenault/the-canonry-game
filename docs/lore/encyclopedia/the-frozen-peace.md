# The Frozen Peace

*Archivist's register: era lens, provisional.*

The Frozen Peace is the polite name for the aftermath of the great orca pressure:
rebuilding, festivals returned with mourning braided into them, cooperative
guilds, chicks treated as arguments against oblivion, and survivors grateful
enough for quiet that they sometimes mistook quiet for repair.

Quillen's colder formulation is more useful: the Frozen Peace is exhaustion
dressed as stability.

## What Remained

Aurora sacred instruments were fractured or missing. Some governance customs
required conditions the war had made impossible and the aftermath had not
restored. Nightshelf secrecy survived, but no longer as untouched exclusivity.
It had been spent under extinction pressure and discovered, to its own surprise,
that an identity could remain after disclosure.

The orca withdrew into waters that were home and interval rather than defeat.
The dead god had not woken. The shamans who remained had learned the cost of
trying. This did not end the theology.

## What Changed Quietly

A crate of aurora-crystals can arrive outside the Accord framework and not be
called kindness because calling it kindness would collapse the accounting. A
Nightshelf ledger can audit a dead worker's three-crystal debt because grief, in
that language, is an imbalance. An Aurora shrine can remain silent because the
object it would have housed has already done worse things in public.

The peace lives in gestures too small for treaty: shared route repairs, names
scratched forward, warm cups set down without questions, a corridor left marked
for someone who may not come back.

## Useful Lore To Extract

- Aftermath should preserve scars as working infrastructure.
- Cooperation can become ordinary without becoming easy.
- The orca threat can recede without resolving.
- Deep corruption should remain where the openings left it.
- Public peace should hide private debts and unfinished mourning.

## Chronicle Use

Use this lens only when an aftermath needs a name. Do not make other entries
depend on the Frozen Peace as a fixed era. The reusable lore is quieter:
survivors resume ordinary life while living among unrepaired consequences.
