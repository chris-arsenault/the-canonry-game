# The Unspoken

*Archivist's register: faction entry.*

The Unspoken are the Nightshelf's most deniable ruling presence: council,
absence, tradition, threat, and useful fiction depending on which document is
being read and who has permission to admit the document exists. Some records call
the same governing layer the Shadow Parliament. Others use neither title and
refer only to effects.

To ask who sits among the Unspoken is to misunderstand the point. A name is a
handle. Power without handle is harder to seize.

## Method

The Unspoken communicate through intermediaries, dead drops, routed rumors,
ritual silences, and decisions that appear first as changed access. A passage
opens. A broker refuses an otherwise profitable deal. A fire-core shipment loses
three units and gains two guards. A witness forgets which question they meant to
ask.

This kind of government is easy to caricature and difficult to replace. It can
move quickly where public councils would still be establishing standing. It can
also convert necessary silence into permanent unaccountability.

## Relationship To Other Powers

The Unspoken require Fire Keepers, brokers, Claws, guilds, and ordinary tunnel
laborers far more than any official admission suggests. Hidden rule still needs
warm corridors, repaired doors, and penguins willing to carry messages without
finishing their own names aloud.

## Chronicle Use

Use the Unspoken when a decision should be felt before it is named. Do not
over-explain them. Their narrative force is the gap between visible consequence
and unprovable origin.
