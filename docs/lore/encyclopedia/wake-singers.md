# Wake-Singers

*Archivist's register: culture and faction entry.*

The Wake-Singers are orca shamans, war-singers, theologians, and deep divers
organized around the body beneath the Corpse Current. They believe the dead god
is not sleeping in the gentle sense. It is waiting to be fed, called, answered,
or released. Their disagreement concerns what mercy will look like when it
rises.

Penguin records often describe the Wake-Singers as a cult. This is convenient
and not false. It is also incomplete. They are a discipline of patience, a
multi-generational intelligence network, a ritual economy of blood, and the only
major force on the Berg that consistently treats the deep as a destination
rather than a resource or secret.

## Belief

Orthodox Wake-Singers teach that accumulated sacrifice will wake the god and
remake the Frozen Sea into an endless hunting ground. Reformists whisper that
waking the god will end everything, and that ending may itself be mercy.
Grandmother Deep has given both factions enough words to keep them obedient and
not enough words to make them certain.

Their songs are not ornament. Hunt-litanies can carry terrain, timing, pod
roles, sacrifice counts, and theological argument in the same resonance. A battle
plan sung as worship is still a battle plan.

## Penguin Adherents

The Wake-Singers are almost entirely orca. The doctrine, the songs, the
descent, the sacrifice arithmetic, the named pods, and the operational reach
are pod work and pod inheritance. Penguin records that frame the Wake-Singers
as a cross-species movement misread the basic shape of the thing.

A small number of penguins take up the doctrine anyway. They are heretics by
every category their own cultures will accept: Aurora penguins who have walked
away from witnessed life for songs no aurora-light can record, Nightshelf
penguins who have surrendered incomplete names for rites whose obedience
requires whole names spoken in the deep. They are rare, they are not loved at
home, and the pods do not refuse them. Wake-Singer doctrine treats theology as
the structuring element, not species, and a penguin who can dive long enough
and sing well enough is a penguin the doctrine can use.

The cost is steeper for penguin bodies. Few last more than a handful of cycles.
Those who do tend to disappear quietly into the deep, leaving behind chronicle
gaps that their original cultures have learned not to investigate.

## Implements

The Dreaming Tooth is the best known Wake-Singer relic: a tooth larger than an
orca calf, said to come from the dead god's jaw, humming at pressure-depth
frequencies and making the water around it behave as if watched. The Ultima Core
and other Before Times objects are sometimes interpreted as pieces, names, or
heartbeats of the same body.

Blood offerings at the Corpse Current accelerate the current enough for even
skeptics to stop speaking for a moment.

## Strengths And Costs

The Wake-Singers excel at pressure-depth, long preparation, acoustic mapping,
and turning loss into further obligation. They can make defeat devotionally
productive, which makes them difficult to deter.

Their cost is consumption. Shamans spend themselves as sacramental fuel.
War-leaders accept bodily alteration that does not stop when the campaign ends.
Pod-bonds can be asked to carry more theology than any living bond was built to
hold.

## Chronicle Use

Use Wake-Singers when the story needs the sea to have memory of its own, when a
raid is also liturgy, or when an apparently irrational act becomes coherent only
under a timescale longer than penguin politics. Do not solve the dead god through
them. They are interpreters, not proof.
