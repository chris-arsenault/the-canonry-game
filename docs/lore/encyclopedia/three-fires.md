# Three Fires

*Archivist's register: metaphysical entry.*

The peoples of the Berg speak as though magic were one subject with local
accents. The record argues otherwise. Aurora-light, captured fire, and
pressure-depth are three separate bargains. They do not combine cleanly, and the
cost of confusing them has supplied several vaults with sealed reports.

## Aurora-Light

Aurora-light belongs most clearly to the Stack. It is the discipline of
perception, preservation, pattern, distant seeing, and memory made visible. Its
medium is aurora-crystal. Its use is slow. It answers questions and rarely solves
the condition that made the question necessary.

Its cost is clarity of self.

## Captured Fire

Captured fire belongs most clearly to the Nightshelf. It is the discipline of
heat, force, shaping, rupture, forge-work, tunnel-work, weapon-work, and the
practical arrogance of cracking a fire-core because freezing is not an argument.

Its cost is flesh. Clean burns teach. Ragged burns accuse. Veteran forge-masters
carry their craft in thickened flipper-pads, stiff joints, feathers that will not
regrow, and a calm familiarity with pain.

## Pressure-Depth

Pressure-depth belongs most clearly to the orca. It is the discipline of deep
water, communion, subsonic speech, directed weight, and the long inward distance
that follows a dive too deep to explain to those who remained near air.

Its cost is interior distance. Old practitioners answer slowly because some part
of them is still below.

## Chronicle Use

Use the three fires as incompatible truths, not colors of the same spellbook.
When a character reaches for power, name the medium and the cost. The cost should
shape behavior before it becomes spectacle.
