# Courier Corps

*Archivist's register: faction entry.*

The Courier Corps is the living circulatory system of the Flipper Accord. Its
members carry fire-cores, aurora-crystals, sealed messages, hostage delegations,
tribunal summons, apologies too formal to be sincere, and warnings too urgent to
wait for sincerity.

Couriers are recruited from both major penguin cultures and then made to belong
fully to neither. This is protection and punishment in the same ink.

## Marks

Courier ink glows faintly in light and dark. It cannot be disguised for long,
which is why it works. A marked courier can pass where another penguin would be
searched, taxed, threatened, recruited, or killed. A marked courier also has no
plausible private life on either side of the route.

Many abandon cultural naming conventions and take trade-names: Ferryman,
Splitpath, Downdraft, Inkwell. A simple name is easier to shout across a calving
crack and harder to hold hostage through ancestry.

## Work

Couriers know the practical Berg better than councils do. They know which
pressure ridge opened early, which tunnel gang changed allegiance, which
ambassador has begun drinking too much, and how many skeletons a new recruit can
pass before looking down.

The tribunal's seventh seat is often drawn from the corps because both cultures
trust couriers more than they trust each other. The couriers find this both
useful and insulting.

## Chronicle Use

Use couriers when a story needs motion between incompatible worlds. A courier
can carry plot without belonging to either side of it. Their best conflicts are
professional: what must be delivered, what must not be read, and what cannot be
ignored after reading anyway.
