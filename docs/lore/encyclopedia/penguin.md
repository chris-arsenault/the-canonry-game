# Penguin

A named penguin. Penguins are physically mobile agents: wardens, brokers,
witnesses, exiles, couriers, witnesses of the ice, and defectors between
cultures.

## Subtypes

- `warden`
- `broker`
- `witness`
- `exile`

## Attributes

- `vitality`: health; reaches zero and the penguin becomes historical.
- `echo`: outward capacity; what the penguin can leave in the world.
- `warmth`: sustaining need and embodied survival pressure.

## Low-Altitude Behavior

Penguins move on the physical plane. They explore, visit settlements, trail
rivals, fish in maritime terrain, and follow artifacts.

Penguins also influence the semantic plane through witness, works, and
chorus-breaking behavior.

## High-Altitude Role

Penguins can be targets or actors for gambits such as wielding artifacts and
relationship-changing actions. Generators may name exiles, leaders, witnesses,
or historical figures when world state makes them significant.
