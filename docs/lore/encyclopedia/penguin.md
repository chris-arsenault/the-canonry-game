# Penguin

*Archivist's register: people entry, general.*

Penguins are the Berg's most numerous speaking people and the ones most likely
to pretend that records, debts, names, and tools can make a lethal world
orderly. The pretense is not foolish. It has kept chicks warm through Long Dark,
kept fire-cores moving through hostile corridors, and kept the dead from being
lost entirely. It has also produced several elegant disasters.

Most penguins belong by birth and training to either the Aurora Stack or the
Nightshelf, though border settlements, courier service, exile, mixed descent,
and necessity make cleaner taxonomies look better in ledgers than in life.

## Common Roles

Colony records often describe penguins by the face they present in a crisis:

- **Wardens** hold doors, routes, warming-chambers, shrines, or laws.
- **Brokers** move goods, secrets, favors, access, and apologies.
- **Witnesses** carry memory into a form others can use or dispute.
- **Exiles** have been forced out, walked out, or survived the loss of the place
  that would have named them.

These are not permanent castes. A warden may become an exile by holding the
wrong door too long. A broker may become a witness by keeping one receipt that
everyone else needs erased. A witness may become dangerous by telling the truth
under conditions where truth has no office.

## What Makes A Penguin Significant

A penguin becomes worth chronicling when private life touches public pressure:
the courier who carries nine fire-cores where twelve were promised, the auditor
who notices a fracture inside the numbers, the miner who hears knocking below a
sealed ledge, the child who picks up a fallen dagger because no adult remains
standing.

Aurora penguins seek witness and accumulated echoes. Nightshelf penguins seek
useful incompletion and protected names. Penguins who move between those logics
are often the ones history finds impossible to file.

## Chronicle Use

When naming a penguin, give them a culture, a pressure they understand, and a
pressure they misunderstand. Avoid making them only an office. The Berg is moved
as often by tired caretakers, careful clerks, embarrassed negotiators, and hungry
chicks as by luminaries and brokers.
