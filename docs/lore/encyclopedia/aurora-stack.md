# Aurora Stack

*Archivist's register: culture entry.*

The Aurora Stack is the sunlit face of Aurora Berg: crystalline spires, trade
terraces, huddle warrens, vent-warmed foundations, and towers cut to catch the
aurora before the light wastes itself on sky. Its people are penguins of witness,
procedure, public memory, and accumulated names.

The Stack's oldest civic claim is simple: the ice remembers. A deed witnessed by
ice, crowd, and record becomes part of the world. A deed performed alone may
still have happened, but it has not yet joined history.

## The Witnessed Life

Aurora identity is additive. Names grow by echoes, deeds, mentorship, and the
regard of those who can read the ice. A young penguin may be known by one name
and one small parenthetical echo. A figure of consequence may carry so many
annotations that speaking the full name becomes a ceremony.

The Stack does not understand secrecy as privacy first. It understands secrecy
as danger to continuity. If no one can witness an action, no one can correct,
honor, remember, or inherit it. This belief produces generous public records,
crowded naming rites, spectacular civic ceremonies, and an administrative
machinery capable of documenting its own paralysis with exquisite clarity.

## Social Shape

The Stack arranges itself vertically because the Berg gave it height and the
aurora rewards exposure. The Apex houses ice-seers, luminaries, and high
auditors. The Trade Terraces hold merchants, crafters, and the Icebound
Exchange. The Huddle Warrens keep the many alive through shared warmth. The
Foundation Depths house vent workers, under-archivists, old tunnels, and truths
too heavy for the upper air.

Status is called prominence. It is not supposed to be inherited. In practice,
old echoes can cast long shadows even under clear light.

## Strengths And Costs

The Stack excels at memory, legitimacy, public trust, long records, and the
conversion of experience into civic continuity. It can gather witnesses quickly.
It can make a death matter. It can turn a single observed fracture into an audit,
a debate, a revised charter, and a ceremony before the ice has finished settling.

Its weakness is the same instrument in colder weather. Procedure can delay
judgment. Consensus can distribute responsibility until no flipper holds it.
Aurora-light answers questions, but answers do not warm chicks or stop teeth at
the waterline.

## Chronicle Use

Use Aurora characters when a story needs reputation, witness, public argument,
ancestral pressure, beautiful records, or the tragedy of a system that performs
properly past the point where proper performance helps. Do not make every Aurora
penguin serene. The Stack contains merchants, miners, exhausted caretakers,
ambitious auditors, failed mystics, and children who know the Counting of Names
before they understand arithmetic.
