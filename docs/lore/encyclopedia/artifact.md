# Artifact

A named object whose importance is large enough for the simulation to track:
relic, instrument, weapon, tome, or seal.

## Subtypes

- `relic`
- `instrument`
- `weapon`
- `tome`
- `seal`

## Attributes

- `condition`: health; reaches zero and the artifact becomes historical.
- `power`: outward capacity.
- `resonance`: internal drive and magical pressure.

## Low-Altitude Behavior

Artifacts are physically mobile in a loose sense: they circulate, seek bearers,
return to shrines, draw toward scars, and sink seaward. They also exert semantic
influence through channeling, devotion, and corruption.

## High-Altitude Role

Artifacts are common targets for generators and gambits. They can sunder
regions, yield tomes, draw factions into conflict, or become wielded by named
penguins.
