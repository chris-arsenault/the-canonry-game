# Before Times Artifacts

*Archivist's register: artifact family entry.*

Before Times artifacts are high-technology weapons from the civilization that
existed immediately before the Collapse. They retain manufactured serial
numbers in technical systems no current culture can read and use materials that
refuse present-day explanation. They do not enter the three cultural naming
systems cleanly. Aurora echoes fail to settle on them. Nightshelf truncation
finds no whole name to hide. Orca deed-names cannot claim what they did not kill.

Their common names are useful and probably unsafe.

## The Known Six

The catalogues most often name six: the Prime Shard, the Extinction Node, the
Ultima Core, the Omega Hammer, the Singularity Obelisk, and the Singularity
Beam. Formal records preserve their serials because the serials persist even
when common names vanish from ledgers, dreams, or memory.

Each object appears to be part tool, part wound, part message. Some absorb
light. Some unmake ice. Some heat without depletion. Some show every reader a
different impossibility. All of them make archivists overuse the word "perhaps."

## Lost History

The weapons were used in the apocalyptic war that killed the Dead God, broke
the magical balance, and caused the Deep Freeze. The civilization that made and
deployed them did not survive intact, so current cultures no longer know this
history. They interpret the weapons as debris from outside the sky, future
echoes, pieces of the Dead God, or works of a civilization much older than their
actual makers. Those theories describe present ignorance, not the weapons'
canonical origin.

The serials imply a series. A series implies a count. No one has admitted to
knowing the number.

## Chronicle Use

Use Before Times artifacts sparingly. They should not feel like powerful local
relics with stranger paint. They should make local categories fail and cause
competent handlers to disagree about whether custody is protection, theft, or
being counted.
