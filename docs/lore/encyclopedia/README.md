# Encyclopedia

*Archivist's register: index and usage note.*

These entries are public-facing lore notes for players and working notes for
agents that need to generate names, places, records, and tensions in the world of
Aurora Berg. They should read as though compiled inside the setting by a
skeptical archivist with access to too many sealed strata.

The entries are gameplay adjacent, not gameplay exact. They may name kinds of
people, factions, places, artifacts, or pressures that the game can express, but
they should not repeat engine attributes, card text, health names, or exact
generator outcomes.

## Use

- Prefer in-world nouns: witness, route, seal, debt, huddle, fire-core,
  aurora-crystal, pod, ledger, echo.
- State what a thing does to life on the Berg before explaining its doctrine.
- Keep contradictions alive when the sources disagree.
- Treat era entries as lenses over remembered history. Do not require other
  entries to depend on those eras remaining fixed in the final setting.
- Leave seed-specific people, places, and artifacts for later seed entries unless
  they are needed to explain a major culture or world force.
