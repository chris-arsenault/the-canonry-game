# Flipper Accord

*Archivist's register: political force entry.*

The Flipper Accord is the mutual hostage arrangement by which the Aurora Stack
and the Nightshelf keep each other alive. The polite version calls it trade. The
accurate version names the dependency: the Stack needs fire-cores from below,
and the Shelf needs aurora-crystals from above. Neither can produce the other's
material. Both can die of the other's absence.

The Accord does not create trust. It creates routes where trust would be too
expensive.

## Terms Everyone Knows

The pressure-ice slab at Midway Shelf holds the public clauses: exchange ratios,
courier guarantees, hostage delegations, arbitration, sanctions, and the old
promise that trade caravans are inviolable. The seventeen skeletons at Midway
Shelf are the most persuasive commentary on the courier guarantee.

Ambassadors live as honored guests and acknowledged hostages. They throw good
parties because fear tastes better with warm food.

## Terms The Ice Knows

Several private drafts, secret renegotiations, and provisional replacements are
preserved in administrative ice. They show the Accord doing what all durable
instruments do: hiding the compromises that allow the public text to keep its
face. At times the Accord allocated abundance. At other times it allocated
deprivation with the same formal dignity.

Its deepest truth is not in any clause. Neither colony can afford to let the
other die.

## Chronicle Use

Use the Accord whenever survival and resentment need to share a table. It is a
route, a court, a market, a hostage ledger, a ration formula, a social fiction,
and one of the few reasons the Berg has not already split into colder graves.
