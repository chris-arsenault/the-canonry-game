# The Deep Freeze

*Archivist's register: catastrophe and present-climate entry.*

The present world is in an artificial ice age. It was not always an ice world.
Before the Collapse, its climate was roughly comparable to Earth's, with
ordinary continents and oceans, temperate ecologies, and civilizations built
for something other than permanent planetary ice.

The apocalyptic war killed the Dead God and destroyed the balance among the
world's magical forces. The resulting climatic catastrophe is called the
**Deep Freeze**. Its duration is unknown. Its onset buried cities, roads,
megaliths, weapons, and whole landscapes beneath advancing ice and destroyed
pre-Collapse civilization as a continuous order.

## The Present Date

The Deep Freeze has ended; the ice age has not. Game year 0 begins roughly
twenty years after the acute catastrophe released its hold. Coasts and oceans
have reopened unevenly, isolated warmth supports life, and survivors have begun
to reconnect, but the world remains glaciated far beyond its natural climate.

Grandmother Deep, Shadow-Broker Nyla, and High-Beak Auditor Selka lived before
the Collapse. They are not merely historians of the old world: each carries
direct memory of some part of it into the seed state.

## What The Ice Covered

The ice lies over a temperate world's geography. Old river basins, eroded
mountain systems, continental margins, wetlands, and coastlines still shape the
present terrain even where glaciers or sea ice conceal them. Cold is therefore
an overprint with history beneath it, not the world's original geological
condition.

The Before Times belong to this same pre-Collapse period. Their serial-numbered
artifacts are manufactured high-technology weapons used in the war that killed
the Dead God. Their apparently alien character records lost technical culture,
not an unrelated ancient civilization.

## Chronicle Use

Use the Deep Freeze as recent civilizational trauma with geological scale. A
present settlement may occupy a thawed fragment of an old metropolis; a glacier
may follow a pre-Collapse road valley; a relic weapon may retain a serial number
whose logistics system vanished with its operators. Survivors can remember the
old world while disagreeing about the war, the god, and what their own factions
did.
