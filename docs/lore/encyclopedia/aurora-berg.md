# Aurora Berg

*Archivist's register: world entry.*

Aurora Berg is the only known home: a continent-sized iceberg, a drifting world,
a body of ice large enough to contain mountains, seas, caverns, trade routes,
grave layers, and civilizations that have never found its edge. It is called
"the Berg" by those who have no need to impress children with geography.

The Berg is not a stable platform on which history happens. It is one of the
participants. It remembers, thaws, cracks, seals, conducts songs, carries heat,
raises old things, and sometimes yields exactly where everyone had been taught
it would hold.

## Shape

The sunlit face rises into the Aurora Stack. The shadowed interior descends into
the Nightshelf. The open waters around it belong to orca claim, hunger, and
current. Beneath those waters lies the Corpse Current. Beneath the working
levels lie deep strata whose impressions predate all known people.

The Berg knows Endless Day and Long Dark, with ruinous transitions between. The
Thaw opens routes and exposes mistakes. The Freeze closes routes and exposes
arithmetic.

## The Only Home

No documented expedition has crossed the Frozen Sea to another land. The longest
serious records end in food calculations, not arrival. This matters more than
most philosophies. The cultures cannot settle conflict by leaving each other
behind. If the Stack freezes, the Shelf pays. If the Shelf falls, the Stack pays.
If the pods starve, the ice-edge pays.

## The Body Hypothesis

Some records call the Berg a vault. The deeper records make "body" harder to
avoid. Things sealed below move upward when pressure changes. Old wounds pulse.
The Glow-Fissure answers gaps in containment. The ice-memory records without
judging, which is not the same as being inert.

## Chronicle Use

Use Aurora Berg as constraint, witness, and pressure. Every local story should
know what the cold is doing, where the warmth comes from, what route can close,
and what old layer may be closer than the characters think.
