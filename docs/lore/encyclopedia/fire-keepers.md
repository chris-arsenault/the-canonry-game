# Fire Keepers

*Archivist's register: faction entry.*

The Fire Keepers control the knowledge, extraction, allocation, and ritual
language of captured fire in the Nightshelf. They are the reason many tunnels
remain warm enough for politics. They are also the reason warmth often arrives
with terms attached.

No one on the Berg mistakes fire-cores for luxury. Control the cores and you can
heat nurseries, fuel forges, light ceremonies, maintain barriers, stop a route
from freezing, or let a rival discover what principle feels like at minus sixty.

## Work

Fire Keepers mine, process, ration, teach, regulate, and conceal. They know which
veins burn clean, which crack sideways, which are already spoken for, and which
old shafts were sealed because the output became difficult to describe.

Their doctrine has not remained pure. Records preserve an older teaching: what
is shared is power multiplied. Later strata carry the harsher revision: what is
hoarded is power preserved. The same rhythm holds both sentences. This is why
children can recite a corruption before they can recognize it.

## Power

The Fire Keepers tolerate brokers, Claws, guilds, and councils when those bodies
keep routes functioning. They oppose them when route control threatens core
control. In wartime, their logistical decisions can look like strategy. In
peacetime, their strategy can look like accounting.

## Chronicle Use

Use Fire Keepers when warmth needs politics. Their best scenes are not speeches
about power, but allocation: who gets a core, who gets a sliver, who gets a
promise, and who is asked to understand that the vein is empty.
