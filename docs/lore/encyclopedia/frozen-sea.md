# Frozen Sea

*Archivist's register: world entry.*

The Frozen Sea surrounds the Berg in every direction and has never admitted an
edge to any map that returned. During Endless Day it opens into lethal water rich
with krill. During Long Dark it freezes for miles and becomes a road for the
desperate, the disciplined, and those taken by the Walking. During the
transitions it is most dangerous, because it changes its mind while penguins are
still standing on yesterday's answer.

To the Aurora Stack, the sea is boundary and hazard. To the Nightshelf, it may
be wall or prison. To the orca, it is home, route, hunting ground, grave, and
history.

## States

In open months, fishing parties go out because hunger gives courage a schedule.
Orca patrol the currents and remember every colony that extends too far. In the
breaking weeks, pressure ridges rise overnight and old channels vanish. In Long
Dark, the ice invites walking. Some who answer return changed. Some do not
return. Some are found smiling.

Shore-watch rotations exist because the horizon can wear down a mind. Stare too
long at a line that offers no change and the body begins inventing a reason to
cross it.

## Currents And Claims

The Corpse Current is the sea's most impossible fact: warm where no vent feeds
it, copper-scented, regular, and guarded by Wake-Singer secrecy. The Hunting
Currents belong to orca memory before any penguin charter. The Killing Shallows
are named honestly by everyone except official colony maps.

## Chronicle Use

Use the Frozen Sea when the world needs a boundary that is also a presence. It
can feed, isolate, tempt, erase, deliver predators, preserve secrets, or make a
penguin realize that "away" is not the same as "elsewhere."
