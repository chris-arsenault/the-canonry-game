# Council of Luminaries

*Archivist's register: faction entry.*

The Council of Luminaries is the Aurora Stack's visible governing body: elected
by demonstration, legitimated under aurora-light, surrounded by auditors,
clerks, witnesses, precedent, and the civic conviction that public procedure can
turn power into service.

When the sky is clear, the Council can move with beautiful confidence. When the
sky is clouded, or the fire-cores required for a public demonstration are in
someone else's ledger, legitimacy becomes cold very quickly.

## Method

The Council prizes consensus, demonstration, public evidence, and witnessed
decision. Its best decisions bind because every penguin can see how they were
made. Its worst decisions bind for the same reason.

Luminaries are not all mystics, though no Luminary survives long without knowing
how mysticism, trade, weather, and crowd attention braid into authority. A
governor who cannot read the room will soon be read by it.

## Failure Pattern

The Council's great danger is not ignorance. It is informed inaction. Records
from several crises show competent subordinates present, figures tallied,
anomalies flagged, votes held, and no mechanism by which one properly alarmed
penguin could halt the instrument.

## Chronicle Use

Use the Council when legitimacy itself is at stake. Let it be neither foolish nor
easy to bypass. The Council should make bad outcomes more tragic by showing that
many good procedures were honored on the way there.
