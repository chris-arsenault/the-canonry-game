# Midnight Claws

*Archivist's register: faction entry.*

The Midnight Claws are the Nightshelf's most persistent answer to the question
of who enforces what official structures cannot admit they need enforced. They
are syndicate, route authority, smuggling apparatus, protection racket,
blackmail office, and sometimes the only reason a fire-core shipment reaches the
right door before the nursery freezes.

Calling them criminals is accurate and insufficient. Calling them necessary is
accurate and dangerous.

## Work

The Claws mark territory with scratches that glow faintly in darkness. They move
goods through routes too narrow for sanctioned caravans, disappear threats to
the supply chain, keep blackmail ledgers, and know which public officials prefer
not to ask why a tunnel is suddenly safe.

Several chronicles record their dissolution, trial, confession, remnants, or
return under other names. This is not contradiction so much as evidence that the
function survives every attempt to abolish the office.

## Cost

The Claws have guarded edges where corruption pressed close, taken payment from
what they contained, and buried some of themselves so others could keep walking.
They have also murdered, extorted, lied, and profited from the social fact that
warmth delivered through fear is still warmth.

## Chronicle Use

Use the Midnight Claws when legality and survival have stopped occupying the
same corridor. A Claw scene should carry practical competence, moral debt, and
the possibility that removing the knife may open the wound wider.
