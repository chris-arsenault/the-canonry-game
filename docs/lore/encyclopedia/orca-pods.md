# Orca Pods

*Archivist's register: culture entry.*

The orca are the Berg's waterborne people: hunters, singers, matriarchal pods,
keepers of deed-names, and the only civilization on the Frozen Sea that does
not ask the ice to remember it. Penguins are tempted to call them raiders. This
is true as far as a bite is true of a mouth.

An orca is what the orca has done. The name is history, not decoration. A young
orca carries a short deed. An elder may take minutes to recite. To lie about a
kill is not boasting. It is theft of existence.

## The Pod

A pod is kinship, battle order, memory, apprenticeship, and survival. Most are
small enough that every member knows the sound of every other member's fear.
Leadership usually rests with the eldest female, whose name is longest because
she has carried the most history without being ended by it.

Pod roles appear in deed-names and war-songs:

- **Vor** strike first and fast.
- **Grrul** herd prey toward the kill.
- **Thrak** finish.
- **Zund** watch, track, and coordinate.

These are functions more than offices. A pod under pressure will use any body
that can still move.

## The Hunt

The hunt is sacred because the hunt is how the world admits truth. Strong prey
may be spared, not from mercy, but because strong prey improves the future. Weak
prey may be taken without hatred. The orca ideal is not remembrance. It is
completion: a long name, a pod still fed, and a final Deep Descent on one's own
terms.

Orca politics are easy for penguins to misread because pods do not build halls
or publish charters. Their claims are currents, bones, songs, scars, and the
fact that a hunting ground remains hunted.

## Strengths And Costs

The pods excel at patience, acoustic memory, coordinated movement, deep water,
and reading the perimeter that penguin institutions forget to watch. Their
bodies are wrong for tunnels and perfect for pressure. When they fight in the
Berg, they suffer in architecture made against them and enter anyway.

Their weakness is theology when theology becomes logistics. A hunt can end. A
crusade can keep feeding itself long after every hunter involved has begun to
break.

## Chronicle Use

Use orca as a civilization with interiority, not as weather. They can be enemies,
traders, witnesses, fanatics, tacticians, grieving pod-mates, and elders who
understand a cycle completely without possessing the institutional means to
break it.
