# Dead God

*Archivist's register: cosmological force entry.*

Beneath the Corpse Current lies a body roughly two miles long, organically
complex, unlike any named creature, and not entirely dead. Its heart beats once
every several minutes. The pulse is old enough to appear in deep ice whose age
makes ordinary history look recently hatched.

Whether it is a god is an argument. Whether something is down there is not.

## The Body And Current

The Corpse Current runs warm without volcanic explanation. Orca track it by its
copper scent. Penguins who know too much describe blood that has been waiting a
long time. During Long Dark, the current keeps moving under sealed ice, and pods
use it to survive where surface charts would predict death.

The first confirmed contact dive returned three observations: the body is vast,
it resembles no living creature, and the heart still beats. The diver died soon
after in a descent that looked like grief.

## Interpretations

Wake-Singers believe sacrifice will wake the god. Some expect remade hunting
grounds. Some expect annihilation as mercy. Grandmother Deep answers both views
just enough to keep the rites moving.

Aurora readers near the coast sense a weight distorting the ice-memory. The
official language remains careful. Nightshelf authorities forbid open discussion
with the strictness of those who already know why the topic is dangerous.

## Chronicle Use

Use the dead god as gravitational pressure, not as a solved monster. Its pulse
can shape cults, currents, artifacts, nightmares, and strategic patience without
requiring any character to know what it truly is.
