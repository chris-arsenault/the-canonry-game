# Content Authoring Ledger

This ledger is the planning artifact for generator, gambit, and low-altitude
authoring. A target is ready to implement only when it has a causal row: source
state, topology creator, low-altitude maintainer, generator condensation,
repeat lock, and verification.

## Distribution tuning evidence

The `authoring-distribution-tune-10x5000-20260514` Monte Carlo run is the
current balance baseline for this ledger. It ran 10 seeds for 5000 ticks each;
all 10 runs reached 200 generator fires and reported `map_degenerate=0`.

The authored generator catalog fired between 17 and 160 times per generator
(`claimed_waters_draw_bulls` to `stolen_artifacts_draw_hunters`), a 9.41x
spread. The gambit catalog succeeded between 53 and 507 times per gambit
(`faction_sues_for_peace` to `settlement_opens_trade_route`), a 9.57x spread.
That keeps deliberate rare/common variation while staying under the 10x
distribution target.
