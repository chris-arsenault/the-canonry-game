I recommend an explicit generation radius of 128, rather than pretending the fixed-capacity kernel is unbounded. A complete radius-128 disk contains 49,537 cells per plane, or 99,074 across both planes. Raising map capacity from 16,384 to 131,072 provides a clear fixed bound and headroom.

A. Deterministic latent terrain field
Owning layers: seed/worldgen defines the terrain; Domain/Host compiles and installs it; Kernel only materializes primitive results.

Generate a coherent physical and semantic terrain/subdivision blueprint through radius 128 using the same algorithms that generate the initial heart.
Materialize only radius 18 at startup.
When frontier growth reaches a coordinate, reveal its planned terrain, subdivision, subregion, and continent rather than assigning Tundra/OpenField or cloning the neighboring subdivision.
Scale feature placement and noise frequencies for the larger field, so radius 128 is not merely a stretched version of radius 18.
Store the installed blueprint in fixed primitive arrays. No runtime domain strings, allocation, or worldgen logic enters the kernel.
