A subdued cartographic map of Aurora Berg, rendered as an archival chart
on warm-cream parchment. The Aurora Stack crystalline spire colony
sketched on one tier of the Berg, Nightshelf tunnel labyrinths
crosshatched beneath, the Corpse Current marked as a dark arc of warm
water tracking below the sea-ice. Compass rose in one corner. Faint
hex-grid overlaying the landmass. Echo-script annotations in the margins.
Muted enough that white text overlaid on it stays fully legible — this
is a backdrop, not a feature. Antique atlas aesthetic, civilizationally
aware.
