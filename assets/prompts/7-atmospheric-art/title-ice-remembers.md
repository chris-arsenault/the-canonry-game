A wide cinematic landscape of Aurora Berg under perpetual twilight. An
Aurora Stack vertical crystalline spire colony rises on the horizon, its
prismatic lenses channeling aurora light down through the tiers. The
aurora itself dominates a cold slate sky, rippling green-white across the
upper half. In the foreground, a single robed ice-seer stands on a ridge
of blue glacial ice, looking toward the colony. Below, at the water line,
the dark hint of the Corpse Current flows past an orca's dorsal fin
barely breaking the surface. Civilizational scale, reverent, grim, quiet.
