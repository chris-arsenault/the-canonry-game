A stylized alliance / compact faction marker. Flat app-icon geometry.
Two rectangular banners crossed in an X shape, mounted together at
the center. Each banner is a different saturated palette primary color
so the alliance reads as two parties joined. A simple circular binding
ring at the center where the banners cross, in a contrasting palette
color. No fly-tails, no sigils — the two-color crossed form IS the
identity.
