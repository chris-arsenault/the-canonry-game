A Before Times relic held in an Aurora Stack deepest vault — an
impossible shard of uncertain material whose surface shifts between
states, edges impossibly sharp. Serial numbers ("8H-H1Y0B") are incised
into its face in faintly phosphorescent script in a language no one alive
can read. Resting on a carved volcanic-glass pedestal. Aurora-crystal
shards arranged in a warding pattern around the pedestal. A cold
wrongness halo around the relic — the ice-memory itself recoils.
