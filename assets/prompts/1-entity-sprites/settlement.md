An Aurora Stack city rising in tiers against a darkening sky — many
crystalline ice spires, not few, reading at civilizational scale: a
population of thousands evident in bridges, ledges, council platforms at
the top, dense Huddle Warren terraces in the middle, Foundation Depths
vents glowing warm ember at the base. Aurora above. Seen from a slight
3/4 elevated angle. Never a hut, never a few domes.
