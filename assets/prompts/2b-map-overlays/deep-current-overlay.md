# Deep Current Travel Structure Overlay

A stylized deep-current overlay marker for a hex map. Simplified app-icon geometry.

Three bold parallel wave strokes sweep diagonally through the icon center, with two small arrowhead fins indicating directed water flow. Use flat saturated palette colors, sharp clean edges, generous padding. The shape should read as a travel lane through water, distinct from road dashes and distinct from terrain texture marks. No ocean background, no scene, no gradients.
