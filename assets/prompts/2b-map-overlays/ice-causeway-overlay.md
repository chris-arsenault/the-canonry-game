# Ice Causeway Travel Structure Overlay

A stylized ice-causeway overlay marker for a hex map. Simplified app-icon geometry.

Three interlocking rectangular ice slabs form a short bridge path across the icon center. The slabs should step slightly from lower-left to upper-right, with two tiny dark crack lines and small triangular notches to make them read as ice blocks, not a road. Flat saturated palette colors, sharp clean edges, generous padding. This represents a built physical travel structure over difficult or blocked terrain.
